# Commands — Player
**▸ Player** (18) · Queue (12) · Playlist (3) · Filters (1) · Settings (2) · General (2)

**?seek <position>**
Jump to a position in the current track

**?forward [seconds]**
Jump ahead, 10 seconds by default

**?rewind [seconds]**
Jump back, 10 seconds by default

**?replay**
Start the current track again

**?sleep [duration]**
Stop the music later, or at the end of this track

**?volume [level]**
Show or set the playback volume

**?grab**
Send what is playing to your messages

**?nowplaying**
Show the Now Playing panel
