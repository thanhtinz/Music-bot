# Commands — Queue
Player (18) · **▸ Queue** (12) · Playlist (3) · Filters (1) · Settings (2) · General (2)

**?queue [page]**
Show the queue, or find a track in it

**?remove <position>**
Take one track out of the queue

**?move <from>**
Move a queued track to another position

**?jump <position>**
Play a queued track now, skipping past the rest

**?shuffle**
Shuffle the upcoming tracks

**?loop [mode]**
Cycle loop mode: off, track, queue

**?autoplay**
Keep playing related tracks when the queue runs out

**?history**
Show what has already played
