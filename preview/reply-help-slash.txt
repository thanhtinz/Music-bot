# Commands — Player
**▸ Player** (18) · Queue (12) · Playlist (3) · Filters (1) · Settings (2) · General (2)

**/play [query]**
Play a track, a link or an upload

**/playnext [query]**
Queue something at the front, to play next

**/search <query>**
Search, then pick what to play

**/pause**
Pause playback

**/resume**
Resume playback

**/skip**
Skip the current track, or vote to

**/previous**
Go back to the previous track

**/stop**
Stop playback and clear the queue
